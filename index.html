Background

I grew up in Michigan, raised by an elementary school teacher and a dad who coached my baseball teams. Before every game, he'd say: "Have fun, play smart, and be humble." I've tried to carry that into everything I do.

Midwest work ethic, humility, and community were instilled early. At Michigan State (Marketing + Sales Leadership), I found sales and it clicked. The part that drew me in was the problem solving — understanding why a company is in pain, mapping who owns it, and figuring out how to help.

As a BDR at ServiceNow, I developed a sharp eye for where enterprise buying motions get complicated. The handoff between marketing and sales. The gap between what an AE knows and what a buyer actually needs to move. The momentum that disappears between meetings because nothing was documented and nothing was handed off cleanly. I became genuinely interested in those friction points, and what it takes to smooth them out.

That interest led me to build. I started creating AI-powered tools for my team — account research systems, call prep briefs, sequencing frameworks — not because I was asked to, but because the opportunity to make things work better was right in front of me. Over time, those tools were adopted organically by 50+ peers. I completed MIT's certification in Applied Agentic AI for Organizational Transformation to go deeper on how to fix those problems at scale.

Selling into ServiceNow's enterprise accounts also gave me something most BDRs don't get — genuine breadth. I've had real conversations with leaders across IT, HR, Customer Service, Sales, Security, Risk, and Procurement. I understand how each function operates, where they conflict, and how cross-departmental workflows create the problems that enterprise software solves. That context makes me a better seller and a better builder.

It's also what made Echelon AI the obvious next move. Echelon is an AI workforce for ServiceNow teams — built to deliver catalog items, flows, apps, and CMDB work live in your instance in hours, not weeks. It's the kind of outcome I've been selling toward for years, now from the inside. Joining as a Founding BDR means building the GTM motion from scratch: the outreach, the messaging, the systems. That combination of selling and building is exactly where I want to be.
